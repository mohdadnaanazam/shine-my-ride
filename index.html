Our process

01

Inspect & quote

Walk-around assessment of paint, film, and interior condition. Clear package recommendation.

02

Wash & decontaminate

Safe wash, iron fallout removal, and clay as needed so correction starts on a clean surface.

03

Correct & refine

Rubbing and multi-stage polish to level defects and unlock depth in the clear coat.

04

Protect & hand over

Ceramic or Teflon applied to spec, interior finished, final QA under shop lighting.
